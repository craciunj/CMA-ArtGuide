My goals in terms of functionality are the following:

- Create a basic search tool, which will find specificed art based on keywords
- Create an advanced search tool, filtering by certain artist names, timeframes, exhibitions, art locations, etc.
- Have a tile grid view with an art piece "snapshot" using the *tombstone* information from the CMA OpenAccess GET response
- Also toggle a more condensed list view
- Have specific art piece pages
- Have specific artist pages
- Maybe more pages like current exhibitons or locations in the museum
- CMA navigation (how do I get from painting A to painting B)
- Leverage Google Gemini to provide more detailed overview / analysis of an artist, art style, art era, etc. for curious users
- Audio input / output for the LLM in the future
